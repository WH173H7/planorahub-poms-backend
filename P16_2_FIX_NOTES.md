# P16.2 Audit, Calendar & Official Letter Fixes

- Admin Audit Logs now load the global audit feed and include an actor/staff filter plus role/department context.
- Staff Calendar remains scoped to the logged-in staff member's assigned tasks/follow-ups and their own reminders.
- Admin Calendar sees all company tasks/follow-ups plus all reminders, with reminder/task ownership labelled.
- Reminder creation is now audited.
- Official Letters now support a visible PlanoraHub watermark in the editor and full stationery treatment in exported PDFs.
- Official Letters support an optional uploaded signatory image, converted client-side to a compact JPEG, previewed on the page and embedded in the PDF.
- PDF export now repeats the branded header, watermark, rule and footer on each page and uses a human-readable letter date.
